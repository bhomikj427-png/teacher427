# Sources — Final Unified Base

All citations behind `research/` (this canonical base), merging both dives plus the gap-check.
Any claim in `00`–`07` traces here. (The original per-dive lists are preserved under
`../_archive/dive-1-original/sources.md` and `../_archive/dive-2-independent/sources.md`.)

## Foundational reviews & cognitive architecture
- **Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013).** Improving Students' Learning With
  Effective Learning Techniques. *Psych. Science in the Public Interest, 14*(1), 4–58.
- **Soderstrom, N. C., & Bjork, R. A. (2015).** Learning Versus Performance: An Integrative
  Review. *Perspectives on Psych. Science, 10*(2), 176–199.
- **Weinstein, Madan & Sumeracki (2018).** Teaching the Science of Learning. *Cognitive Research:
  Principles and Implications, 3*(2). (Open access.)
- **Sweller, J.; Paas & van Merriënboer (2020).** Cognitive Load Theory. *Current Directions in
  Psych. Science.* (Incl. the germane-load falsifiability critique / its removal.)
- **Bjork & Bjork** — New Theory of Disuse (storage vs. retrieval strength; desirable difficulties).
- **Cowan, N.** WM capacity (~4); **Miller (1956)** "7±2" (historical).

## Retrieval practice & transfer
- **Adesope, Trevisan & Sundararajan (2017).** Meta-analysis of practice testing (217 studies;
  g≈0.5–0.7). *Review of Educational Research, 87*(3).
- **Roediger & Karpicke (2006).** Test-enhanced learning (foundational).
- **Agarwal, Bain & Chamberlain (2012)** and **Agarwal, Nunes & Blunt (2021).** Retrieval practice
  in real classrooms / systematic review. *Educational Psychology Review.*
- **Pan & Rickard (2018).** Transfer of Test-Enhanced Learning: meta-analysis (transfer d=0.40).
  *Psychological Bulletin, 144*(7).

## Spacing, successive relearning & sleep
- **Cepeda, Pashler, Vul, Wixted & Rohrer (2006).** Distributed practice meta-analysis.
  *Psychological Bulletin, 132*(3).
- **Rawson, Dunlosky & Sciartelli (2013).** The Power of Successive Relearning. *Educational
  Psychology Review*; **Rawson & Dunlosky (2022)**, *Current Directions in Psych. Science.*
- **Sleep & memory consolidation** — Active Systems Consolidation framework; slow oscillations &
  spindles; better retention after sleep than wake. (Reviews:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2680680/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12332499/)

## Generation, pretesting & productive failure
- **Richland, Kornell & Kao (2009).** The pretesting effect. *J. Exp. Psych: Applied.*
- **Sinha, T., & Kapur, M. (2021).** When Problem Solving Followed by Instruction Works: Evidence
  for Productive Failure. *Review of Educational Research* (166 comparisons; g≈0.36 corrected).

## Self-explanation / generative learning
- **Bisra, Liu, Nesbit, Salimi & Winne (2018).** Inducing Self-Explanation: a Meta-Analysis (64
  studies; g=0.55). *Educational Psychology Review, 30*.
- **Fiorella, L., & Mayer, R. E. (2015/2016).** *Learning as a Generative Activity* / "Eight Ways
  to Promote Generative Learning," *Educational Psychology Review.* (Summarizing, mapping,
  drawing, imagining, self-testing, self-explaining, teaching, enacting; SOI model.)
- **Fiorella & Mayer — learning by teaching / protégé effect** (preparing-to-teach d≈0.30–0.59;
  actually teaching → best delayed retention).

## Interleaving
- **Brunmair & Richter (2019).** Similarity matters: meta-analysis of interleaved learning
  (g=0.42). *Psychological Bulletin.*
- **Block-first / threshold:** Hwang et al. (2025), *Language Learning*; Kang (interleaved
  category learning); executive-function moderation (PMC10658001).

## Worked examples, fading & concreteness fading
- **Sweller** — worked-example & guidance-fading effects; **Kalyuga** — expertise reversal & ATI.
- **Fyfe, McNeil, Son & Goldstone (2014).** Concreteness Fading in Mathematics and Science
  Instruction: A Systematic Review. *Educational Psychology Review.* (+ Goldstone & Son 2005.)

## Multimedia / dual coding
- **Mayer, R. E.** Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning; 12 principles (multimedia d≈1.67;
  coherence d≈0.86; signaling d≈0.46; etc.). *J. Computer Assisted Learning* (2017).

## Teaching methods
- **Rosenshine, B. (2012).** Principles of Instruction. *American Educator.*
- **Bloom, B. S. (1984).** The 2 Sigma Problem. *Educational Researcher, 13*(6).
- **Nickow, Oreopoulos & Quan (2020).** Tutoring meta-analysis (avg ≈0.37 SD; none 2σ).
- **VanLehn, K. (2011).** Human vs. intelligent tutoring (ITS d≈0.76; human d≈0.79).
- **Vygotsky** — Zone of Proximal Development / scaffolding.

## Assessment & feedback
- **Hattie & Timperley (2007).** The Power of Feedback. *Review of Educational Research, 77*(1).
- **Kluger & DeNisi (1996).** Feedback interventions meta-analysis (131 studies; ~38% negative).
  *Psychological Bulletin, 119*(2).
- **Black & Wiliam (1998).** Formative assessment. **Koedinger & Aleven** — assistance dilemma.
- **Anderson & Krathwohl (2001)** — revised Bloom's taxonomy.

## Metacognition & motivation
- **Zimmerman** — Self-Regulated Learning. Calibration meta-analysis ("Calibrating Calibration");
  metacognitive questioning improves calibration (PMC11570305).
- **Deci & Ryan** — Self-Determination Theory.

## Contested
- **Sisk, Burgoyne, Sun, Butler & Macnamara (2018).** Growth mind-sets: two meta-analyses
  (intervention d=0.08; ~0.02 best studies). *Psychological Science.*
- **Yeager, D. S., et al. (2019).** A national experiment reveals where a growth mindset improves
  achievement. *Nature, 573*, 364–369. (N=12,490; +0.10 GPA for lower-achievers in supportive
  schools.)
- **Macnamara, Hambrick & Oswald (2014).** Deliberate Practice meta-analysis (education: 4% of
  variance). *Psychological Science.*

## Excluded
- **Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer & Bjork (2008).** Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence (no
  meshing evidence). *Psych. Science in the Public Interest, 9*(3).
- **Pomodoro** scoping review (2025), *BMC Medical Education* (outcomes = focus/fatigue, not
  learning).

## Methodological — effect-size inflation
- **"Neglect of publication bias compromises meta-analyses of educational research" (2021),**
  *PLOS ONE.* (Education meta-analyses avg d≈0.40 vs. pre-registered RCTs d≈0.06.)
- **"Meta-analyses in psychology often overestimate evidence for and size of effects."** (PMC10320355.)
