# Sources

Primary research behind the evidence base. Grouped by topic. Effect sizes and study scales are
quoted in files `01`–`05`; this is the citation index so any claim can be traced.

## Foundational reviews / frameworks

- **Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K. A., Marsh, E. J., Nathan, M. J., & Willingham, D. T. (2013).**
  Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From
  Cognitive and Educational Psychology. *Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14*(1),
  4–58. — The canonical rating of 10 techniques by utility.
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1529100612453266 ·
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/
- **Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L.** A new theory of disuse and an old theory of stimulus
  fluctuation (storage strength vs. retrieval strength; "desirable difficulties").

## Cognitive architecture / cognitive load

- **Sweller, J.** Cognitive Load Theory (intrinsic/extraneous/germane load; worked-example,
  split-attention, expertise-reversal, guidance-fading effects).
- **Paas, F., & van Merriënboer, J. J. G. (2020).** Cognitive-Load Theory: Methods to Manage
  Working Memory Load in the Learning of Complex Tasks. *Current Directions in Psychological
  Science.* https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0963721420922183
- **Cowan, N.** Working memory capacity (~4 chunks). / **Miller, G. (1956)** "7 ± 2" (historical).
- Development/refinement of CLT: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-023-09817-2

## Retrieval practice (testing effect)

- **Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017).** Rethinking the Use of Tests:
  A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. *Review of Educational Research, 87*(3). 217 studies;
  g ≈ 0.50–0.61. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/0034654316689306
- **Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006).** Test-enhanced learning. (Foundational.)

## Spaced / distributed practice

- **Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006).** Distributed
  Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A Review and Quantitative Synthesis. *Psychological
  Bulletin, 132*(3), 354–380. 184 articles / 317 experiments.
  https://www.yorku.ca/ncepeda/publications/CPVWR2006.html ·
  https://augmentingcognition.com/assets/Cepeda2006.pdf

## Interleaving

- **Brunmair, M., & Richter, T. (2019).** Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved
  learning and its moderators. *Psychological Bulletin.* Overall g = 0.42.
  https://www.psychologie.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/06020400/2019/Brunmair_Richter_in_press__2019_META-ANALYSIS_OF_INTERLEAVED_LEARNING.pdf
- **Rohrer, D., & Taylor, K.** Interleaving of mathematics practice.

## Self-explanation / elaborative interrogation

- **Bisra, K., Liu, Q., Nesbit, J. C., Salimi, F., & Winne, P. H. (2018).** Inducing
  Self-Explanation: a Meta-Analysis. *Educational Psychology Review, 30*, 703–725. 64 studies;
  g = 0.55. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-018-9434-x ·
  https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1186664

## Pretesting / generation / errorful learning

- **Richland, L. E., Kornell, N., & Kao, L. S. (2009).** The pretesting effect: Do unsuccessful
  retrieval attempts enhance learning? *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied.*
  https://learninglab.uchicago.edu/Pre-Testing_files/RichlandKornellKao.pdf

## Multimedia learning (dual coding)

- **Mayer, R. E.** Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning; 12 research-based principles.
  Multimedia principle median d ≈ 1.67; coherence d ≈ 0.86; etc.
- **Mayer, R. E. (2017).** Using multimedia for e-learning. *Journal of Computer Assisted
  Learning.* https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jcal.12197
- Principles for reducing extraneous processing (coherence, signaling, redundancy, spatial/
  temporal contiguity). https://www.researchgate.net/publication/262915119

## Teaching methods

- **Rosenshine, B. (2012).** Principles of Instruction: Research-Based Strategies That All
  Teachers Should Know. *American Educator.* https://www.aft.org/ae/spring2012/rosenshine
- **Bloom, B. S. (1984).** The 2 Sigma Problem: The Search for Methods of Group Instruction as
  Effective as One-to-One Tutoring. *Educational Researcher, 13*(6), 4–16.
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/0013189X013006004
- **Nickow, A., Oreopoulos, P., & Quan, V. (2020).** The Impressive Effects of Tutoring on
  PreK–12 Learning: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Avg ≈ 0.37 SD; none reached 2σ.
- **VanLehn, K. (2011).** Relative effectiveness of human tutoring, intelligent tutoring
  systems, and other instruction. ITS d ≈ 0.76 vs. human d ≈ 0.79.
- Systematic review of mastery learning / tutoring / direct instruction:
  https://nintil.com/bloom-sigma/ · https://www.educationnext.org/two-sigma-tutoring-separating-science-fiction-from-science-fact/
- **Vygotsky, L.** Zone of Proximal Development / scaffolding.

## Assessment & feedback

- **Hattie, J., & Timperley, H. (2007).** The Power of Feedback. *Review of Educational
  Research, 77*(1), 81–112. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/003465430298487
- **Kluger, A. N., & DeNisi, A. (1996).** The effects of feedback interventions on performance.
  *Psychological Bulletin, 119*(2), 254–284. 131 studies; ~38% of effects negative.
- **Black, P., & Wiliam, D. (1998).** Assessment and Classroom Learning (formative assessment).
- **Koedinger, K. R., & Aleven, V.** The assistance dilemma.
- **Bloom's taxonomy** (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001 revision).

## Metacognition & self-regulation

- **Zimmerman, B. J.** Self-Regulated Learning (forethought → performance → self-reflection).
- Meta-analysis of interventions to improve metacognitive monitoring accuracy ("Calibrating
  Calibration"). https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349179781
- Metacognitive questioning improves calibration, esp. for low achievers:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11570305/

## Motivation

- **Deci, E. L., & Ryan, R. M.** Self-Determination Theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness).

## CAVEATS — included only with warnings

- **Sisk, V. F., Burgoyne, A. P., Sun, J., Butler, J. L., & Macnamara, B. N. (2018).** To What
  Extent and Under Which Circumstances Are Growth Mind-Sets Important to Academic Achievement?
  Two Meta-Analyses. *Psychological Science.* Intervention d = 0.08; ~0.02 in best studies.
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797617739704 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29505339/
- **Macnamara, B. N., Hambrick, D. Z., & Oswald, F. L. (2014).** Deliberate Practice and
  Performance in Music, Games, Sports, Education, and Professions: A Meta-Analysis.
  *Psychological Science.* Education: 4% of variance.
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0956797614535810
  - Ericsson-camp critique (individualization): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-021-02326-x

## EXCLUDED — failed the bar

- **Pashler, H., McDaniel, M., Rohrer, D., & Bjork, R. (2008).** Learning Styles: Concepts and
  Evidence. *Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 9*(3), 105–119. No evidence for the
  meshing hypothesis. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x ·
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26162104/
  - **Nancekivell et al. (2020)** — persistence of the learning-styles belief.
- **Pomodoro** scoping review (2025), *BMC Medical Education*: evidence moderate-to-low;
  outcomes were fatigue/focus/motivation, not learning retention.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12532815/ ·
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12909-025-08001-0

---

*Note on sourcing: effect sizes were drawn from the meta-analyses and reviews above. Where a
range is quoted (e.g. retrieval g ≈ 0.50–0.70), different summaries of the same literature
report slightly different aggregates depending on inclusion criteria; the conservative figure
is used in teaching decisions.*
