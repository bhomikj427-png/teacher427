## 0. Scope & goal
- **Learner goal (stated 2026-11-15):** three equal aims — (1) learn better as a learner, (2) teach
  others well, (3) understand and upgrade this tutor engine. Every topic carries all three uses.
- **Shape:** Part A = the science of learning, organised by *kind of information*. Part B = the
  teachers and method-makers (roster: `roster.md`), each method tied to the Part A information
  type it serves and graded for evidence.
- **Core distinction (learner's framing):** *studying* = sitting down with the intent to learn;
  *learning* = a durable, transferable change in what you can do unaided. Research anchor:
  learning vs performance (Soderstrom & Bjork 2015).
- **Relation to the engine:** `../../../research/` already covers much of Part A as *engine
  method*. It is a seed only — every claim is re-verified at primary source for this KB. Findings
  that would change the engine go to a proposal (design sign-off first), never a direct edit.
