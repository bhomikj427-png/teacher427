# Pedagogy — Source Canon (tiered)

> Tiers (protocol §2): **T1** peer-reviewed primary study / meta-analysis / the author's own
> primary text · **T2** authoritative review book or handbook chapter by the researchers ·
> **T3** reputable translator (researcher-written popular book, researchED-grade practitioner) ·
> **T4** popularizer / YouTube / blog — leads only, every claim re-verified.
>
> **"Verified" column:** `bib` = citation details confirmed this session (journal, vol, pages,
> DOI) from a publisher/PubMed/DOAJ record · `claim` = the specific finding we cite was also
> confirmed from the abstract or full text · `—` = listed, not yet checked. Stage 2 (S11) upgrades
> every load-bearing source to `claim` at full text.
