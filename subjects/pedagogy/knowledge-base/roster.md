## 2. Mid-20th-century method-builders (research-linked)

| Person | Method | Info type | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fred Keller (1899–1996) | Personalized System of Instruction: self-paced units, mastery gate, proctors | factual + procedural | S–M (Kulik et al. 1979; 1990 ≈0.49 SD — verify) | |
| Benjamin Bloom (1913–1999) | Mastery learning; taxonomy; "2-sigma problem" | all; gating | M (mastery ≈0.5 SD); 2σ **not replicated at scale** | Nickow 2020, VanLehn 2011 already in research/ |
| Robert Gagné (1916–2002) | Nine events of instruction; learning hierarchies; types of learning outcome | **directly an info-type taxonomy** | T–M | key bridge for Part A ↔ B |
| David Ausubel (1918–2008) | Meaningful learning, advance organizers ("what the learner already knows") | conceptual | M (Luiten et al. 1980 meta; modest) | ties to Justin Sung's "priming" |
| Jerome Bruner (1915–2016) | Spiral curriculum; enactive→iconic→symbolic (→ Singapore CPA); scaffolding | conceptual | M (via concreteness fading) | discovery-learning claims: contested |
| Siegfried Engelmann (1931–2019) | Direct Instruction: scripted, faultless communication, mastery | procedural + factual | S (Stockard et al. 2018, 328 studies; Project Follow Through) | separate "big DI" from "little di" |
| Toru Kumon (1914–1995) | Self-paced worksheets just above level, mastery repetition | procedural (arithmetic) | M (Bangladesh RCT, independent eval) | |
| Japanese lesson study (Meiji era →) | Teachers co-plan, observe, revise a single lesson | teacher learning | T–M | Stigler & Hiebert *The Teaching Gap* |
| Oxford/Cambridge tutorial | 1–2 students, essay, defend it | conceptual + argumentation | T | links to Bloom 2σ / tutoring meta |
| Paulo Freire (1921–1997) | Critical pedagogy, anti-"banking" model | motivation/social | T (outcome evidence thin) | include, grade honestly |
