## Official syllabus (verbatim)

Experiments carried out on hardware **and** software to analyse the characteristics of
semiconductor devices — diodes, transistors, MOSFETs. Circuits based on these devices are studied
for various device and circuit parameters, on hardware as well as software (SPICE-class
simulation).

## Research note (how Stage 1 treats a lab)

A lab has no separate content syllabus — its theory **is** ECE2101's. Stage 1 for a lab is light:
the knowledge base records (a) the standard experiment set (device I-V characterisation, rectifier/
clipper/clamper circuits, FET/BJT characteristics, MOSFET switching), (b) the measurement method
and expected results per experiment (mechanism: *what each curve should look like and why*), and
(c) common lab pitfalls. Most conceptual depth lives in `../03-electronic-devices-1`; the lab note
cross-references it rather than duplicating it.
