## Official syllabus (verbatim, MUJ ECE Syllabus 2023-onwards)

- **Signals & systems:** introduction, classification, properties.
- **LTI systems (CT & DT):** impulse response & step response; convolution; frequency response and
  its relation to impulse response.
- **Fourier:** Fourier series representation; Fourier transform — properties, magnitude & phase
  response; Discrete-Time Fourier Transform (DTFT) and Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT).
- **Laplace transform (CT):** region of convergence; system functions; poles & zeros; Laplace-domain
  analysis; solving differential equations & system behaviour.
- **z-transform (DT):** eigenfunctions; ROC; system functions; poles & zeros; z-domain analysis.
- **System realization:** block-diagram representation & interconnection; state-space analysis;
  multi-input multi-output representation; state-transition matrix and its role.
- **Sampling:** Sampling Theorem & implications; spectra of sampled signals; reconstruction — ideal
  interpolator, zero-order hold, first-order hold; aliasing & its effects; relation between
  continuous- and discrete-time systems.
