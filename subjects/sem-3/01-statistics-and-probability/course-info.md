## Official units (UNVERIFIED — reconcile with the official handout)

1. **Probability theory & random variables** — probability basics; random variables; CDFs;
   discrete & continuous RVs; independence; pmf/pdf; expectation; Chebyshev's inequality; central
   limit theorem.
2. **Probability distributions** — Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Normal, Exponential.
3. **Theory of estimation** — maximum likelihood & method-of-moments estimation; sufficient
   statistics; Bayesian estimation; confidence intervals for means.
4. **Tests of statistical hypothesis** — parameter vs statistic; standard error; hypotheses;
   critical region; significance; Type I / Type II errors; tests about one/two means; tests of
   variances; chi-square test; ANOVA.
