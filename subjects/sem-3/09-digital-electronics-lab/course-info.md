- **Code:** ECE2131  ·  **L-T-P-C:** 0-0-2-1  ·  Semester 3, B.Tech ECE, MUJ

## Official syllabus (verbatim)

Experiments implemented at hardware **and** software level. List includes: implementation of
combinational and arithmetic circuits using logic gates and MSI chips; design of sequential
circuits; and implementation of FSMs for their applications.

## Research note (how Stage 1 treats a lab)

Theory is ECE2102's. Stage 1 records the standard experiment set (gate-level verification,
adders/subtractors, mux/decoder, flip-flops, counters, shift registers, FSM implementation), the
relevant 74-series MSI chips and their pinouts, expected behaviour per experiment (mechanism), and
common pitfalls (floating inputs, contact bounce, clock/skew issues). Conceptual depth lives in
`../04-digital-electronics`; this note cross-references it. Connects to `../../verilog` for the HDL
route to the same circuits.
