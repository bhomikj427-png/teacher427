## Official syllabus (verbatim, MUJ ECE Syllabus 2023-onwards)

- **Operational amplifiers:** function & characteristics of the ideal op-amp; differential & common-
  mode signals; block-level representation.
- **Linear applications:** weighted amplifier; converter circuits; design of differentiator &
  integrator; instrumentation amplifier; bridge amplifier.
- **Non-linear applications & filters:** active filters — design & analysis of low-pass, high-pass,
  band-pass, band-elimination and all-pass; rectifiers; peak detector; sample & hold; comparators;
  window detector; Schmitt trigger; square- & triangular-wave generators; oscillators.
- **Timer IC 555:** pin details & internal working; applications — multivibrator, Schmitt trigger.
- **Data converters:** DAC & ADC principles and specs; binary-weighted & R-2R DAC; successive-
  approximation, counter-type, servo-tracking, and dual-slope ADC.
- **PLL & VCO:** IC 565 (PLL) and IC 566 (VCO) — analysis & applications.
- **IC voltage regulators and power amplifiers.**
- **Lab:** hardware + software on linear/non-linear op-amp applications; PLL, VCO, timer IC circuits.
