## How dates change scheduling (the rule, evidence-aligned)

- **Normal mode (no date near):** expanding intervals as usual (successive relearning,
  `research/02 §2–§3`).
- **Exam taper (date known, approaching):** intervals *tighten* into the final pre-exam week so
  every exam-scoped item gets a last successful retrieval close to the exam — this is still
  spacing (the material was learned earlier), **not** cramming; cramming = first-time learning
  compressed, which stays forbidden.
- **Priority under time pressure:** `exam-map.md` frequency × weakness (items with recent failed
  retrievals first). Scoring-over-depth governs — Stage-2 depth pauses inside the taper window.
- **After the exam:** items return to normal expanding intervals (durable learning is the goal;
  the exam is a milestone, not the endpoint).
