## Official syllabus (verbatim, MUJ ECE Syllabus 2023-onwards)

- **Network theorems & elements:** superposition; Thevenin's & Norton's theorems; maximum power
  transfer; networks with dependent sources.
- **Transient analysis:** impulse, step, ramp & sinusoidal response of first- and second-order
  circuits; time-domain & transform-domain (Laplace) analysis; initial & final values of networks.
- **Two-port networks:** Z, Y, hybrid (h), and ABCD parameters and their interrelations;
  equivalence of two-ports; interconnection of two-ports; filters; image impedance; symmetric T and
  π networks.
- **Network functions:** terminals & terminal pairs; driving-point impedance/admittance & transfer
  functions; finding network functions for general two-terminal-pair networks; stability &
  causality; Hurwitz polynomial; positive real function.
- **Network synthesis:** the four reactance-function forms; specifications for a reactance function;
  Foster forms; Cauer forms; synthesis of R-L, R-C, and L-C networks in Foster and Cauer forms.
- **Lab:** hardware + software experiments to analyse circuits & networks.

## Prescribed textbooks (official)

2. Sudhakar & Shyammohan — *Circuits and Networks* (5e), TMH, 2017.
3. Hayt, Kemmerly & Durbin — *Engineering Circuit Analysis* (8e), McGraw-Hill, 2012.
4. Ashfaq Husain — *Networks and Systems* (2e), Khanna, 2021.
5. Salivahanan & Pravin Kumar — *Circuit Theory*, Vikas.
