## ⚠ The title is misleading — read this first

Despite the name "**Management of Technology**," this is **not** the classical MOT course (technology
forecasting, S-curve strategy, R&D portfolio management, tech-transfer economics). The official
**topic list, all three reference books, and the prescribed NPTEL course are entrepreneurship /
business-management material.** Treat this subject as an **Entrepreneurship + business-functions +
IPR survey**, framed for engineers. The NPTEL course the scheme links is literally titled
*"Entrepreneurship Management."* This reframing drives everything downstream (and the Stage-2
question — see `knowledge-base/00-map.md`).

## Official topics (verbatim from the scheme — flat list, no unit numbers given)

> Invention and Innovation; Market analysis; Technology transition; Market survey technique;
> Analysing marketing opportunities; Ansoff Matrix; Project Formulations (1, 2 and 3) based on market
> survey technique; Commercialization; Financial Management; Human Resource Management; Leadership;
> Intellectual Property Rights.

**NPTEL link (in the scheme):** *Entrepreneurship Management* —
`https://onlinecourses.swayam2.ac.in/cec24_mg28/preview` (SWAYAM cec24_mg28).

### Engine-imposed unit grouping (NOT official — for KB organization only)

The scheme gives a flat topic list. The KB groups it into 5 logical units for teaching/spacing.
This grouping is **ours**, marked `uncertain` until a PYQ or course handout confirms the real
unit/MTE-ETE split:

1. **Innovation & technology foundations** — Invention vs Innovation; Technology transition.
2. **Market analysis & marketing** — Market analysis; Market survey technique; Analysing marketing
   opportunities; Ansoff Matrix.
3. **Entrepreneurship & project formulation** — Project Formulations (1, 2, 3); Commercialization.
4. **Functional management** — Financial Management; Human Resource Management; Leadership.
5. **Intellectual Property Rights** — the IP families + Indian terms/acts.

## Prescribed references (verbatim)

1. V. S. P. Rao, *Business, Entrepreneurship and Management*, 1st ed., New Delhi: Vikas
   Publications, 2010. — **primary textbook** (broadest coverage of the topic list).
2. D. F. Kuratko & R. M. Hodgetts, *Entrepreneurship: Theory, Process and Practice*, 9th ed.,
   Boston: Cengage Learning, 2016. — entrepreneurship process / commercialization.
3. R. Roy, *Entrepreneurship*, 1st ed., New Delhi: Oxford University Press, 2008.
