# MBB2101 — Predictable Misconceptions (Stage 1)

> Novice errors to pre-empt in teaching (protocol §4). Sourced to the discipline's standard framing
> where possible; this is a survey course so most are *definition confusions*, not deep
> misconceptions. Each confidence-marked.

- **M1 — "Invention = innovation."** The single most common error and the course's whole point.
  Invention = the new idea/device; innovation = invention **commercialized/put to use**. `settled`.
  → *Fix:* an idea in a notebook vs the same idea sold profitably.
- **M2 — "Diversification is just 'doing more stuff,' low risk."** It's the **highest-risk** Ansoff
  quadrant (new product **and** new market — least existing knowledge applies). `settled`.
- **M3 — "Market penetration means entering a new market."** No — penetration = **more sales of
  existing products in existing markets**. *Market development* is the new-market one. Easy swap to
  make under exam pressure. `settled`.
- **M4 — "A patent gives worldwide protection."** Patents are **territorial**; an Indian patent
  protects only in India (international filing via PCT). `settled`.
- **M5 — "Copyright must be registered to exist."** Copyright arises **automatically** on creation;
  registration only aids evidence. (Contrast patents, which *require* grant.) `settled`.
- **M6 — "Trademarks expire like patents."** Patents end at 20 yr (no renewal); trademarks are
  renewable **indefinitely** in 10-yr blocks. A brand can outlive any patent. `settled`.
- **M7 — "Profit maximization is the goal of financial management."** Modern view: **wealth/value
  maximization** — profit maximization ignores **timing, risk, and cash flow**. `settled`.
- **M8 — "IRR is always the best decision rule."** IRR can mislead (multiple/again-no IRR with
  non-conventional cash flows; ranking conflicts with NPV). **NPV is the theoretically preferred
  rule.** `settled`.
- **M9 — "Leader = manager."** Overlapping but distinct: managers use positional authority and
  control; leaders use influence/vision. One can exist without the other. `settled`.
- **M10 — "Primary data is data you find first / online."** Primary = collected **first-hand for this
  study** (survey/interview); secondary = **pre-existing** data. The name confuses students.
  `settled`.
- **M11 — "Qualitative vs quantitative = small vs big sample."** The split is **type of data/insight**
  (why vs how-many), not sample size. `settled`.
- **M12 — "Break-even is about profit."** Break-even is the point of **zero profit** (revenue =
  total cost); it's the *floor*, not a profit target. `settled`.
