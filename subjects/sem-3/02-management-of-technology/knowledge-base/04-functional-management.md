## A. Financial Management

**Objective:** maximize the **value of the firm / shareholder wealth** (preferred over "profit
maximization," which ignores timing and risk). `settled`

**Core decisions (the 3):** **investment** (which assets — capital budgeting), **financing** (debt
vs equity mix — capital structure), **dividend** (pay out vs retain). `settled`

**Sources of finance:**
- **Equity** (owner's funds, shares — no fixed repayment, ownership dilution) vs **Debt** (loans,
  debentures — fixed interest, repayable, tax-deductible, but raises risk).
- Short-term (working capital, trade credit) vs long-term (equity, term loans, debentures).

**Time Value of Money (the threshold idea):** ₹1 today > ₹1 later (opportunity + risk + inflation).
- Future value: `FV = PV(1 + r)^n`. Present value: `PV = FV / (1 + r)^n`. `settled` (verified
  standard formulas).

**Capital budgeting (project appraisal) — the examinable methods:**
- **Payback period** — years to recover the initial outlay. Simple; ignores TVM & post-payback cash
  flows.
- **NPV** = Σ [Cashflowₜ / (1+r)ᵗ] − initial investment. **Accept if NPV > 0.** Best single rule
  (uses TVM, absolute value added). `settled`
- **IRR** — the discount rate making NPV = 0. **Accept if IRR > cost of capital.** Can mislead with
  non-conventional cash flows. `settled`
- **(ARR** — accounting rate of return; ignores TVM.)
- **Break-even point** = Fixed cost / (price − variable cost per unit) = FC/contribution. Units to
  cover all costs. `settled` (verified).

**Working capital** = current assets − current liabilities; finances day-to-day operations.

> Exam note: if a PYQ shows numeric NPV/IRR/payback/break-even sums, this is the one place to add
> worked problems (the "Stage 1.5" finance item from `00-map.md`).

## B. Human Resource Management
Managing people to achieve organizational goals. **Core functions (exam list):**
- **HR planning** — forecast demand/supply of people.
- **Recruitment & selection** — attract a pool, then choose (tests, interviews).
- **Training & development** — build current + future capability.
- **Performance appraisal** — measure & feedback (links to feedback/motivation).
- **Compensation & benefits** — pay, incentives, rewards.
- **Industrial/employee relations** — grievances, discipline, unions, welfare.
`settled`. Sometimes split into managerial (planning/organizing/directing/controlling) vs operative
(the functions above) functions.

## C. Leadership
- **Manager vs leader:** manager relies on position/authority and *does things right* (planning,
  control); leader relies on influence/vision and *does the right things*. `settled`
- **Theories (exam-standard set):**
  - **Trait theory** — leaders born with traits (intelligence, confidence, drive). Critique: traits
    don't reliably predict.
  - **Behavioural** — leadership is *what you do*: task-oriented vs people-oriented (Ohio State,
    Blake-Mouton managerial grid).
  - **Contingency/situational** — best style *depends on the situation* (Fiedler; Hersey-Blanchard
    situational leadership — adjust to follower maturity).
- **Styles:** **autocratic** (leader decides), **democratic/participative** (team involved),
  **laissez-faire** (hands-off). `settled`
- **Motivation (links here):** **Maslow** (need hierarchy: physiological→safety→social→esteem→
  self-actualization), **Herzberg** (hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction vs motivators create
  satisfaction), **McGregor Theory X** (people dislike work, need control) **vs Theory Y** (people
  are self-motivated). `settled`.
