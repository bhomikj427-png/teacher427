# Unit 2 — Market Analysis & Marketing (Stage 1)

> Official topics: *Market analysis; Market survey technique; Analysing marketing opportunities;
> Ansoff Matrix.* Sources: Rao (primary); standard marketing-management framing (Kotler-lineage
> frameworks are textbook-universal). `settled` unless marked.

## 1. Market analysis
Assessing whether/where a market exists for the offering:
- **Market size & potential** — total addressable demand; growth rate.
- **Segmentation basis** — geographic, demographic, psychographic, behavioural.
- **Demand assessment** — current + forecast demand (survey, trend, expert methods).
- **Competition** — number/strength of rivals; substitutes; barriers to entry.
- **Customer** — needs, willingness to pay, buying behaviour.

## 2. Market survey technique (likely a high-yield exam topic — it's named twice in the scheme)
Collecting market data systematically.

- **Data types:** **Primary** (collected first-hand: surveys, interviews, observation, experiments)
  vs **Secondary** (already exists: reports, census, industry data). Primary = costlier/more
  relevant; secondary = cheaper/faster/may be stale.
- **Qualitative** (depth interviews, focus groups, open questions — *why*) vs **Quantitative**
  (structured questionnaires, scales — *how many/how much*).
- **Survey process:** define objective → design instrument → choose sampling → collect → analyse →
  report.
- **Sampling:** **probability** (random, stratified, cluster, systematic — allows statistical
  inference) vs **non-probability** (convenience, judgement, quota, snowball). Links to
  Statistics & Probability (subject 01) — sampling/estimation. `settled`
- **Questionnaire design:** clear, unbiased, non-leading; mix of closed (scaled) + open items;
  pilot before full run.

## 3. Analysing marketing opportunities (the framework cluster)
- **STP:** **Segmentation → Targeting → Positioning.** Split market → choose segment(s) → craft a
  distinct position in the customer's mind.
- **Marketing mix — the 4 Ps:** **Product, Price, Place, Promotion** (services extend to 7 Ps:
  +People, Process, Physical evidence). `settled`
- **Environmental scan — PESTEL:** Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal —
  the macro forces. `settled`
- **SWOT:** Strengths, Weaknesses (internal) × Opportunities, Threats (external). Used to match
  internal capability to external opportunity. `settled`
- (Often paired) **Porter's 5 forces** for industry attractiveness — `likely` on syllabus by
  association; include if PYQ shows it.

## 4. Ansoff Matrix (verified — a near-certain exam item)
Growth-strategy grid: **products (existing/new) × markets (existing/new)** → four strategies, in
ascending risk:

| | Existing product | New product |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing market** | **Market Penetration** (sell more to current customers — lowest risk) | **Product Development** (new products to current customers) |
| **New market** | **Market Development** (current products to new markets/segments) | **Diversification** (new products + new markets — highest risk) |

- **Mechanism / threshold idea:** the matrix is a **risk ladder** — risk rises as you move away from
  what you already know (existing product + existing market). Diversification can be *related*
  (synergy) or *unrelated/conglomerate* (highest risk).
- **Exam:** "Draw the Ansoff matrix and explain each quadrant with an example" — high-frequency
  format for this kind of course. → render the 2×2 to the lesson surface (primitive 5, table).
