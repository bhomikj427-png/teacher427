# Unit 5 — Intellectual Property Rights (Stage 1)

> Official topic: *Intellectual Property Rights.* The one topic with hard, examinable facts
> (acts, terms) and real correctness stakes — so it's verified to higher confidence. Terms
> web-verified 2026-06-24 (Lexology, India-Briefing). For teaching, re-confirm against the **bare
> Acts** (the Stage-1.5 hardening flagged in `00-map.md`).

## 1. What IP is — the threshold idea
IP = legal rights over **creations of the mind**. An IP right is a **time-limited monopoly granted in
exchange for public disclosure** (or for protecting goodwill). This *bargain* — incentive to create
vs eventual public benefit — explains every term length and every exclusion. Not a reward; a deal.
`settled`.

## 2. The IP families in India + governing acts + terms (verified)

| IP type | Protects | Indian Act | Term (India) |
|---------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Patent** | New, non-obvious, useful **inventions** | Patents Act **1970** | **20 years** from filing date; **no renewal** |
| **Copyright** | Original literary/artistic/musical/software works | Copyright Act **1957** | Author's **life + 60 years** |
| **Trademark** | Brand marks (names, logos, signs) | Trade Marks Act **1999** | **10 years**, renewable **indefinitely** (each 10 yr) |
| **Industrial Design** | Aesthetic/visual design of an article | Designs Act **2000** | **10 years + 5 year extension = 15 yr** max |
| **Geographical Indication** | Goods tied to a place (Darjeeling tea) | GI of Goods Act **1999** | **10 years**, renewable `likely` |
| Trade secret | Confidential business info | (no dedicated act; contract/common law) | Indefinite while secret |
| Semiconductor layout-design | IC topographies | SICLD Act 2000 | 10 years |
| Plant varieties | New plant varieties | PPV&FR Act 2001 | varies (15–18 yr) |

All term figures `settled` (web-verified 2026-06-24) **except GI renewal mechanism = `likely`**.
Administered by the **CGPDTM** (Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks) under DPIIT.

## 3. Patents — the most-tested sub-topic
- **Patentability tests:** must be (1) **novel** (new worldwide), (2) **non-obvious / inventive
  step**, (3) **industrially applicable / useful**, and (4) **not in the excluded list**.
- **Not patentable (Indian Patents Act §3, key exclusions):** mere discoveries / laws of nature,
  mathematical or business methods, computer programs *per se*, methods of agriculture, methods of
  medical treatment, mere admixtures, traditional knowledge. `settled` (general); §-numbers `likely`.
- **Process:** file application (provisional/complete) → publication (18 months) → request for
  examination → examination/objections → grant → renewal fees. Patent is **territorial** (Indian
  patent ≠ protection abroad; see PCT for international filing).

## 4. Copyright vs Patent vs Trademark (the classic compare question)
- **Copyright** protects *expression*, not ideas; arises **automatically** on creation (registration
  optional but useful for evidence).
- **Patent** protects *functional inventions*; requires **registration**; strongest but hardest +
  time-limited.
- **Trademark** protects *brand identity/goodwill*; renewable **forever**; ∴ a brand can outlive a
  patent.

## 5. Why IP matters to this course
Ties Unit 1 (invention) → Unit 3 (commercialization): an engineer's invention is an asset only if
**protected** before disclosure/commercialization. IP can be **commercialized** by licensing,
assignment, or franchising — a route to market without manufacturing. `settled`.

- "Differentiate patent / copyright / trademark."
