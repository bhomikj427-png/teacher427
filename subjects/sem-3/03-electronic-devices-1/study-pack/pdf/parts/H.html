No ECE 2106 end-term paper or blueprint has been seen, so the real format is unknown. This paper copies the layout of the one official blueprint in hand for this session (MAS2001, July–Nov 2026): Part A 10 × 2 marks, Part B 6 × 6 marks, Part C 2 × 8 marks, Part D 1 × 8 marks, total 80. Questions marked "sheet" are from the professor's numerical sheets and were not used earlier in this pack.

Part A · 10 × 2 = 20 marks

  1. Define mobility and give its unit.
  2. State the mass-action law.
  3. Give two reasons why silicon is preferred over germanium for diodes and transistors.
  4. Define peak inverse voltage. What is the PIV of each diode in a bridge rectifier?
  5. Give two differences between Zener breakdown and avalanche breakdown.
  6. Define pinch-off voltage of a JFET.
  7. When a reverse gate voltage of 15 V is applied to a JFET, the gate current is 10⁻³ μA. Find the resistance between gate and source. sheet Q6
  8. Why is an enhancement MOSFET called a "normally-off" device?
  9. Write the relation between α and β. Find β when α = 0.99.
  10. State the Barkhausen criterion.

Part B · 6 × 6 = 36 marks

  1. Derive the expression for the conductivity of a semiconductor. Find the resistivity of intrinsic germanium at 300 K, given ni = 2.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻³, μn = 3800 cm²/V·s and μp = 1800 cm²/V·s.
  2. Draw the circuit of a centre-tapped full-wave rectifier and explain its working with waveforms. Its transformer secondary is 9 V–0–9 V rms and the load is 1 kΩ. With ideal diodes, find Vdc, Idc and the PIV of each diode.
  3. A D-MOSFET amplifier has VDD = +15 V, RD = 620 Ω, the gate returned to ground through RG = 10 MΩ (so VGS = 0), the source grounded, and a load RL = 8.2 kΩ coupled to the drain through a capacitor. The D-MOSFET has IDSS = 12 mA and gm = 3.2 mS. Determine (i) d.c. drain-to-source voltage VDS and (ii) a.c. output voltage. Given vin = 500 mV. sheet Q29
  4. A voltage-divider bias circuit has VCC = 16 V, R1 = 62 kΩ, R2 = 9.1 kΩ, RC = 3.9 kΩ, RE = 0.68 kΩ and β = 150. Using the approximate method find VB, IC and VCE. Then find the stability factor S.
  5. In a single stage amplifier the h-parameters are hic = 1.1 k, hrc = 1, hfc = −51, hoc = 25 μA/V. Calculate AI, AV, AVS, Ri and Ro for the CC configuration with RS = RL = 10 k. sheet (h-parameters) Problem 3
  6. Draw the block diagram of a negative-feedback amplifier and derive its gain. An amplifier has a gain of 60 dB and an output impedance of 10 kΩ. Voltage-series feedback with β = 0.05 is applied. Find the gain with feedback (as a number and in dB) and the new output impedance.

Part C · 2 × 8 = 16 marks

  1. Define the four characteristic parameters of a JFET, stating what is held constant in each. Derive μ = gmrd. Starting from Shockley's equation, show that gm = gm0(1 − VGS/VGS(off)).
  2. With a circuit diagram, explain the RC phase-shift oscillator and state the conditions for oscillation. Design one for 1 kHz using capacitors of 0.01 μF.

Part D · 1 × 8 = 8 marks

  1. (a) An E-MOSFET is biased by a voltage divider: VDD = 24 V, R1 = 100 kΩ (top), R2 = 15 kΩ (bottom), RD = 470 Ω, source grounded. The data sheet for this particular MOSFET gives ID(on) = 500 mA at VGS = 10 V and VGS(th) = 1 V. Determine VGS and VDS. sheet Q31 (b) Draw a two-input CMOS NAND gate and explain its operation with a truth table.

Key: mock paper

  1. Drift velocity per unit electric field, μ = vd/E; cm²/V·s. (A4)
  2. In equilibrium n·p = ni², whatever the doping. (A2)
  3. Larger band gap, so far smaller reverse leakage current; works at higher temperature (about 150 °C against 75 °C); higher PIV; cheap and forms a good oxide (any two). (A1, B2)
  4. The largest reverse voltage across a diode while it is not conducting. Bridge: Vm. (B5)
  5. Zener: heavy doping, thin depletion region, field pulls electrons from bonds, below about 5 V, negative temperature coefficient. Avalanche: light doping, wide region, carrier multiplication by collision, above about 6 V, positive coefficient. (B8)
  6. The drain-to-source voltage, at VGS = 0, at which the channel pinches off and ID levels off at IDSS. Equal in magnitude to VGS(off). (C1)
  7. RGS = VGS/IG = 15 V/10⁻⁹ A = 1.5 × 10¹⁰ Ω = 15 000 MΩ.
  8. It has no channel at VGS = 0, so no drain current flows until VGS exceeds VT. (D1)
  9. β = α/(1 − α); β = 0.99/0.01 = 99. (E2)
  10. |βA| = 1 and total loop phase shift 0° or 360°. (G1)
  11. J = q(nμn + pμp)E = σE (A4). σ = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 2.5 × 10¹³ × (3800 + 1800) = 0.0224 (Ω·cm)⁻¹; ρ = 1/σ = 44.6 Ω·cm.
  12. Circuit and working: B5. Vm = 9√2 = 12.73 V (tap to one end). Vdc = 0.636Vm = 8.10 V. Idc = 8.10 V/1 kΩ = 8.10 mA. PIV = 2Vm = 25.5 V.
  13. (i) Zero-biased, so ID = IDSS = 12 mA. VDS = 15 − 12 mA × 0.62 kΩ = 7.56 V. (ii) AC drain load RAC = RD ∥ RL = 620 ∥ 8200 = 576 Ω. vout = gmRACvin = 3.2 mS × 576 Ω × 500 mV = 922 mV (inverted).
  14. βRE = 102 kΩ ≥ 10R2 = 91 kΩ, so the approximation holds. VB = 16 × 9.1/71.1 = 2.05 V. VE = 1.35 V. IC ≈ IE = 1.35/0.68 k = 1.98 mA. VCE = 16 − 1.98 × (3.9 + 0.68) = 6.92 V. RTh = 62 ∥ 9.1 = 7.94 kΩ, RTh/RE = 11.7; S = 151 × 12.7/(151 + 11.7) = 11.8. (E4)
  15. Same four formulas as E5 with the CC set. AI = −hfc/(1 + hocRL) = 51/1.25 = 40.8. Ri = hic + hrcAIRL = 1.1 k + 408 k = 409.1 kΩ. AV = AIRL/Ri = 0.997. Yo = hoc − hfchrc/(hic + RS) = 25 μS + 51/11.1 k = 4.62 mS, so Ro = 216 Ω. AVS = AVRi/(Ri + RS) = 0.974. An emitter follower: gain just under 1, very high Ri, low Ro.
  16. Diagram and derivation: F1. 60 dB means A = 1000. 1 + βA = 51. Af = 1000/51 = 19.6 = 25.8 dB. Zof = 10 k/51 = 196 Ω.
  17. C3 in full. For the last part: dID/dVGS = 2IDSS(1 − VGS/VGS(off))(−1/VGS(off)) = gm0(1 − VGS/VGS(off)) with gm0 = −2IDSS/VGS(off) = 2IDSS/|VGS(off)|.
  18. Circuit and explanation: G2. Conditions: three RC sections give 180° at f0 = 1/(2πRC√6), the inverting amplifier gives the other 180°, and |A| ≥ 29. Design: R = 1/(2π × 1000 × 0.01 × 10⁻⁶ × 2.449) = 6.50 kΩ; Rf = 29R = 188 kΩ.
  19. (a) VGS = VG = 24 × 15/115 = 3.13 V. K = 500 mA/(10 − 1)² = 6.17 mA/V². ID = 6.17 × (3.13 − 1)² = 28.0 mA. VDS = 24 − 28.0 mA × 470 Ω = 10.8 V (saturation: 10.8 ≥ 2.13 ✓). (b) D5: two pMOS in parallel from VDD to the output, two nMOS in series from the output to ground. Output is 0 only when both inputs are 1 (both nMOS on, both pMOS off); otherwise at least one pMOS pulls it to 1.
