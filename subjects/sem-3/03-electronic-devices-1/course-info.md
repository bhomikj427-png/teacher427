## Official syllabus (verbatim, MUJ ECE Syllabus 2023-onwards)

- **Semiconductor physics fundamentals:** intrinsic & extrinsic semiconductors; energy bands in
  intrinsic/extrinsic silicon; carrier transport — diffusion current, drift current, mobility,
  resistivity; sheet resistance.
- **PN junctions:** formation, depletion region, forward/reverse bias; generation & recombination;
  Poisson & continuity equations; I-V characteristics; small-signal & switching models; diode
  circuits — rectifiers, clippers, clampers; avalanche breakdown; Zener diode; Schottky diode.
- **JFETs:** construction & operation; characteristics & applications.
- **MOSFETs:** fundamentals; n-channel & p-channel; characteristics & regions of operation;
  small-signal analysis; common-source/-gate/-drain configurations; MOSFET as a switch & role in
  digital systems; MOSFET-based digital circuits.
- **BJTs:** construction & operation; characteristics; comparison with MOSFETs.
