## Slide: "Characteristics parameters of JFET" (verbatim)

1. **Mutual conductance or transconductance, g_m**
   g_m = (∂I_D/∂V_GS) at constant V_DS = ΔI_D/ΔV_GS, with V_DS constant
2. **Drain resistance, r_d**
   r_d = (∂V_DS/∂I_D) at constant V_GS = ΔV_DS/ΔI_D, with V_GS constant
3. **Drain conductance, g_d**
   g_d = (∂I_D/∂V_DS) at constant V_GS = ΔI_D/ΔV_DS, with V_GS constant
4. **Amplification factor, μ**
   μ = −(∂V_DS/∂V_GS) at constant I_D = −ΔV_DS/ΔV_GS, with I_D constant

Teaching content (incl. μ = g_m·r_d): `../knowledge-base/03-jfets.md`, "The four characteristic parameters".
