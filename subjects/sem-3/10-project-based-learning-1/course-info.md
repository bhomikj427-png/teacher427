## Official syllabus (verbatim)

"Based on the identification of a research problem / latest innovation and literature review.
Evaluation will be based on the report and presentation."

## Research note (how the engine treats PBL)

There is **no subject content** to research to a textbook depth here. The knowledge base for PBL is
a light **method** note, not a content base:
- how to identify and frame a research problem / scope an innovation,
- how to do a literature review (find, triangulate, and cite sources — mirrors
  `../../../subject-research-protocol.md` §1–§5 at student scale),
- how to structure the report and the presentation, and the evaluation rubric expectations.

The actual *project topic* is the learner's choice (often drawn from one of the other Sem-3
subjects), so this folder stays mostly a scaffold until a topic is chosen. Lowest research priority.
