# MEE2003 Engineering Economics — Course info

Source: `exam-pack/HANDOUT-MEE2003-course-handout-2026.pdf` (MUJ, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, session 2026-27).
`settled` for everything below unless marked.

- **Code / LTPC:** MEE2003 · 3-0-0-3 · offered to all engineering branches. Decks still carry the old footer "ME2001" / "ME 2003".
- **Coordinator:** Dr Ramanpreet Singh. Decks: Dr Sanjeev Jakhar (L2–L19), Dr S S Sharma (L1).
- **Assessment:** MTE 30 · CWS 30 (quiz, assignment) · ETE 40. 75% attendance required for the ETE (L1 deck).
- **MTE boundary:** printed after **L24**. MTE = L1–L24. The ETE adds budgeting, risk vs return, depreciation (SLM, declining
  balance, double declining, sinking fund) and value analysis (L25–L36).
- **COs:** .1 decision-making concepts and types of estimates · .2 financial viability of projects · .3 budget and budgetary
  control · .4 replacement and break-even analysis · .5 feasibility by risk analysis.
- **Syllabus:** Concept and Value Analysis, Economic Decision Making, Types of Estimates, Accounting and Control, Elements of
  Cost, Prime Cost, Overheads, Types of Cost, Process Cost & Cost of Production, Break Even Analysis, Inventory Control &
  Management, EOQ, Financial Analysis, Simple payback, ROI, NPV, IRR, Life Cycle Cost Method, Sensitivity Analysis, Project
  Financing Options, Budget and Budgetary Control, Types of Budgets, Risk vs Return, System Analysis & System Engineering,
  Value Analysis, Replacement Analysis, Depreciation.
- **Textbooks:** T1 R. Panneerselvam, *Engineering Economics*, PHI · T2 Riggs, Bedworth & Randhawa, *Engineering Economics*,
  McGraw Hill · T3 P.L. Mehta, *Managerial Economics*, Sultan Chand.
- **References:** Grant, Ireson & Leavenworth (Wiley) · Thuesen, Fabrycky & Thuesen (PHI) · Blank & Tarquin (McGraw Hill).
- **Decks received (2026-09-25):** L1–L19. **Missing:** L20–L24 (cost, LCC, project financing, break-even) and everything
  after the MTE. No assignment, no PYQ.
