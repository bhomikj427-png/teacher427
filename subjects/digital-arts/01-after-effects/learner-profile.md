# After Effects — learner profile

> Current level, goals, misconceptions to watch, and subject-specific preference overrides. Maintained by
> the engine via the self-update protocol (`../../../CLAUDE.md`). Created 2026-09-28.

## Goal
- [stated 2026-09-28] Learn the layout, how to operate the software, the shortcuts and "whatever
  usefulness" in **one night**, and leave with **an end product**, even unfinished. Not aiming at mastery.
- Part of a new `digital-arts` group; After Effects is its first subject.

## Current level — undiagnosed (default: novice)
- No evidence yet of prior After Effects use. Treated as novice: worked steps first, then the learner
  repeats them (ROUTE = procedure → worked example first).
- First probes (when a live session happens): has the learner used any layer-based editor (Photoshop,
  Premiere, Canva)? Can they say what a keyframe is? Those decide how fast chapters 1–2 go.

## Misconceptions to watch
From `knowledge-base/misconceptions.md`: T1 anchor point, T2 stopwatch deletes keyframes, T3 arrows vs
frame step, T6 shape vs mask, T12 Lossless render size. Confirm by what happens in the build, not assume.

## Preferences (overrides)
- [engine note 2026-09-28] **Question base is empty for this subject.** The universal question-first rule wants
  questions quoted from assignments or a textbook. There are no assignments, and Adobe's guide has no
  exercises. → Cards open with a **"predict, then try it in the software"** prompt taken from the guide's
  own procedures, labelled as a try-it, never dressed up as a sourced exam question. The guess-first
  mechanism (pretesting) stays.
- Hands-on replaces written answers here: "attempt" = do it in After Effects, then check.

## Tensions to manage
- [stated 2026-09-28] **"One good night and learn most of it" vs principle 2 (spacing; cramming is the
  anti-pattern).** One night gives fast performance, and much of it fades without spacing. Honoured: the
  night is organised around a **build** (generation, not reading), which is the strongest use of one
  session. Mechanism kept: the curriculum schedules day +1 / +3 / +7 returns so the night's work is
  retrieved, not just done once. Named to the learner in one line.
